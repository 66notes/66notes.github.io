How to play

  • Take turns. Tap your dice and your token moves that many squares.
  • Land at the bottom of a ladder and you climb up.
  • Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.
  • First to reach square 50 wins. If you roll too high, you bounce back by the extra steps.
