How to play

  • Move the slider to place your striker.
  • Drag backwards anywhere on the board, then let go to shoot. Drag farther for more power. Drag back to the start to cancel.
  • Pocket all your coins to win. Pocket one of yours and you shoot again.
  • Queen (red): after pocketing it, pocket one of your coins in the same or next shot, or it returns.
  • The Queen must be covered before anyone's last coin can be pocketed.
  • Foul: if the striker is pocketed, one of your coins comes back and your turn ends.
