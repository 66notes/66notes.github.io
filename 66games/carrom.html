Carrom

How to play

  • Each game, colours are given out at random. White breaks first.
  • Move the slider to place your striker, then drag backwards on the board and let go. Drag back to the start to cancel.
  • Pocket all your coins to win. Pocket one of yours and you shoot again.
  • Queen (red): pocket one of your coins in the same or next shot to cover it, or it returns.
  • The Queen must be covered before anyone's last coin can be pocketed.
  • Foul: if the striker is pocketed, one of your coins comes back and your turn ends.
  • Points: the winner gets 1 for each coin the other player has left, plus 3 more for covering the Queen.
